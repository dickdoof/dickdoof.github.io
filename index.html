What I'm building

finHero – Stripe to DATEV export

Automatic Stripe DATEV export for German, Austrian and Swiss companies. It covers bookings, fees, refunds, payouts, receipts and PRAP. Guide: Stripe Buchhaltung in Deutschland. There's also a Claude plugin for DATEV exports.

SepaHeld – SEPA direct debit for your ERP

Collects SEPA direct debits straight from your ERP via GoCardless. It's event-driven on Kafka and integrates multiple ERP platforms.

NPS-Hero – Net Promoter Score surveys

Measure customer loyalty and collect feedback with NPS surveys.
